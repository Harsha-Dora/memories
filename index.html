A Letter For You

Some things deserve more than a text message.

September 2026

To my first friend ❤️,

From the little kids we once were to the people we are today… you’ve been a part of my life for so long that it’s hard to imagine my story without you in it.

You were my first friend, and somehow, life took us through different phases. Something happened, things changed, and we went our separate ways for a while. But somehow, after all those years, life brought us back together again. 🫂

And just when I thought we had found our way back to each other, my own mistake the one you already know about made us stop talking again. I know I’m the reason things are this way, and I’m truly sorry.

I just want you to know that, no matter how much life changes or how far apart we become, I'll always be grateful that you were and always be the first friend my life ever gave me.

Happy Birthday. ❤️

Your Dora
